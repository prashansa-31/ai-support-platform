# Order Tracking Guide

## Purpose
Help customers check the status of an order.

## Instructions
1. Sign in to your account.
2. Open the Orders or Order History section.
3. Select the relevant order to view its current status and any available tracking information.

## If tracking information is missing
Some orders may not display tracking information immediately. Check again later. If the status remains unavailable, contact the support team and provide your order reference.

## Privacy
Only request the order information needed to investigate the issue. Never ask customers to share their account password.
