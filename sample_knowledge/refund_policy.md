# Refund Policy

## Purpose
Explain the basic refund process to customers.

## Requesting a refund
Customers who want a refund should contact the support team with their order reference and a brief explanation of the issue.

## Eligibility
Refund eligibility depends on the product or service purchased and the applicable refund terms. The support team must review the request before confirming whether a refund is available.

## Processing
If a refund is approved, the customer will be informed of the next steps and the expected processing time, where known.

## Important note
This sample article does not define a specific refund window or guarantee approval. Check the applicable terms before promising a refund.
