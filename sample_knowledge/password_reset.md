# Password Reset Guide

## Purpose
Help customers regain access to their account when they forget their password.

## Instructions
1. Open the login page.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the email address registered to the account.
4. Check your inbox for the password-reset email.
5. Follow the link in the email and choose a new password.

## If the email does not arrive
Check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. If the email still does not arrive, contact the support team.

## Security
Never share your password or password-reset link with anyone.
